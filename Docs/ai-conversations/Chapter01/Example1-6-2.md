# Example 1.6.2 - Improved Discovery Prompt and Response

## Prompt

Act as a senior Business Analyst supporting Project Polaris.

Review the initial business request in
"Project Polaris - Initial Business Request":

A government agency wants to create an online grants management
portal for community organisations.

The portal should allow organisations to apply for grants and
track their applications.

Internal staff must be able to review and process applications.

Do not design the technical solution yet.

Prepare a discovery approach that includes:

1. the business objectives that must be clarified
2. the likely stakeholder groups
3. questions for the first discovery workshop
4. assumptions contained in the initial request
5. risks created by missing information
6. functional and non-functional areas that require investigation
7. recommended discovery outputs

Organise the questions under:
- business objectives
- applicant eligibility
- grant programs
- application processing
- assessment and approval
- document management
- security and privacy
- reporting
- accessibility
- support and operations

Identify all open questions explicitly. Do not invent answers.

## Response

I am preparing a structured discovery approach from the initial business request, focusing on clarification needs, stakeholders, assumptions, risks, and explicitly open workshop questions without proposing a technical design.

## Discovery Approach for Project Polaris

### 1. Business objectives to clarify
All of the following are currently open questions that need confirmation.

1. What policy outcomes the grants portal must support.
2. What success measures define value for applicants and internal staff.
3. What service-level expectations apply to application processing timelines.
4. What compliance obligations govern grant administration and record keeping.
5. What scope is in phase 1 versus later phases.
6. What constraints exist for budget, delivery timing, and change management.

### 2. Likely stakeholder groups
1. Sponsoring government agency executives and program owners.
2. Grants program managers and policy officers.
3. Internal assessment, approval, and case processing staff.
4. Finance, audit, and compliance teams.
5. Privacy, legal, and information governance representatives.
6. Security and risk management stakeholders.
7. Reporting and performance management stakeholders.
8. Support operations and service desk representatives.
9. Community organisations applying for grants.
10. Potential partner users such as external assessors or panel members, if applicable.

### 3. First discovery workshop questions
All questions below are open and should be treated as unresolved until validated.

#### Business objectives
- Open question: What are the top business outcomes this portal must achieve in the first 12 to 24 months?
- Open question: Which current pain points are highest priority to fix first?
- Open question: What measurable KPIs will be used to evaluate success?
- Open question: What policy, audit, or ministerial reporting commitments must this initiative satisfy?

#### Applicant eligibility
- Open question: Which organisation types are eligible to apply, and what exclusions exist?
- Open question: What eligibility criteria must be validated before application submission?
- Open question: Are there different eligibility rules by grant program or funding round?
- Open question: What evidence is required to prove eligibility?

#### Grant programs
- Open question: How many grant programs are in scope, and how frequently do rules change?
- Open question: What common versus program-specific fields are required?
- Open question: Are programs open continuously or by fixed rounds?
- Open question: What funding caps, co-funding rules, or constraints must be enforced?

#### Application processing
- Open question: What are the required stages from draft to final decision?
- Open question: What status definitions are needed for applicant tracking?
- Open question: What events trigger staff actions or applicant updates?
- Open question: What turnaround targets exist for each stage?

#### Assessment and approval
- Open question: What assessment criteria and scoring methods are required?
- Open question: Who can assess, moderate, recommend, and approve?
- Open question: Are there conflict-of-interest or segregation-of-duties rules?
- Open question: What approval pathways exist for exceptions or escalations?

#### Document management
- Open question: What documents must applicants and staff provide at each stage?
- Open question: What file types, size limits, and quality standards apply?
- Open question: What retention, archival, and disposal obligations apply?
- Open question: What versioning and traceability expectations exist for submitted documents?

#### Security and privacy
- Open question: What user roles and access boundaries are required?
- Open question: What sensitive data is collected, and what lawful basis governs its use?
- Open question: What privacy notices, consent wording, and data handling controls are required?
- Open question: What audit trail requirements apply to all user and staff actions?

#### Reporting
- Open question: What operational, performance, compliance, and executive reports are mandatory?
- Open question: What dimensions are needed for reporting such as region, program, cohort, or period?
- Open question: What reporting frequency and publication timelines are required?
- Open question: What data quality checks are needed before reporting outputs are trusted?

#### Accessibility
- Open question: What accessibility standard level must be met?
- Open question: What language, readability, and inclusive content requirements apply?
- Open question: What assisted channels are needed for users who cannot self-serve digitally?
- Open question: What accessibility testing and acceptance criteria will be used?

#### Support and operations
- Open question: What support model is required for applicants and internal staff?
- Open question: What hours of support and incident response expectations apply?
- Open question: What operational handoffs are required across business teams?
- Open question: What business continuity expectations apply during peak submission periods?

### 4. Assumptions in the initial request
1. A single portal can support both applicant and internal staff needs.
2. Community organisations can and will use a digital channel for applications.
3. Application tracking is expected by applicants as a core service.
4. Internal review and processing can be standardised to a defined workflow.
5. The agency has authority and readiness to operationalise a new grants process.
6. Data handling, privacy, and security obligations can be met within project scope.

### 5. Risks created by missing information
1. Misaligned scope leading to rework and timeline slippage.
2. Policy non-compliance if grant rules and obligations are not fully captured.
3. Privacy or security gaps if data classifications and controls are undefined.
4. Poor applicant experience if eligibility and document requirements are unclear.
5. Inconsistent assessment outcomes if criteria and approvals are not explicit.
6. Reporting failure if required metrics and data definitions are not agreed early.
7. Accessibility non-compliance if standards and testing approach are not defined.
8. Operational disruption if support model and ownership are not clarified.

### 6. Functional and non-functional areas requiring investigation

#### Functional areas
1. Applicant onboarding, eligibility screening, and profile management.
2. Grant program setup, application intake, and submission lifecycle.
3. Internal triage, assessment, recommendation, and approval processes.
4. Document collection, validation, and records handling.
5. Status tracking, correspondence, and notification obligations.
6. Case management, exception handling, and escalation pathways.
7. Reporting and audit evidence generation.

#### Non-functional areas
1. Privacy, legal, and records compliance obligations.
2. Security governance, access control, and auditability.
3. Accessibility and inclusive service requirements.
4. Service performance expectations, including peak period handling.
5. Availability, continuity, and operational resilience.
6. Usability and supportability for both external and internal users.
7. Data quality, integrity, and retention requirements.

### 7. Recommended discovery outputs
1. Discovery brief with clarified objectives, scope boundaries, and success measures.
2. Stakeholder map with roles, decision rights, and engagement plan.
3. Current-state and target-state process maps at business level.
4. Prioritised requirements catalogue separated into functional and non-functional.
5. Assumptions, dependencies, and constraints register.
6. Risk register with initial mitigations and ownership.
7. Data and information requirement summary including privacy classifications.
8. Reporting requirement matrix with definitions and frequency.
9. Accessibility and compliance requirement baseline.
10. Discovery decision log and open-questions log with owners and due dates.

If useful, I can turn this into a workshop-ready agenda and a facilitation script for the first 90-minute discovery session.
