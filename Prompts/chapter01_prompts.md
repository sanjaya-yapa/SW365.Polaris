# Chapter 01
## Example 1.6 AI in Action 1: Asking Better Questions
### Project Brief
#### Project Polaris – Initial Business Request

A government agency wants to create an online grants management
portal for community organisations.

The portal should allow organisations to apply for grants and
track their applications.

Internal staff must be able to review and process applications.

#### A better way to ask the question
Act as a senior Business Analyst supporting Project Polaris.

Review the initial business request in
Docs/discovery/project-brief.md.

Do not design the technical solution yet.

Prepare a discovery approach that includes:

1. the business objectives that must be clarified
2. the likely stakeholder groups
3. questions for the first discovery workshop
4. assumptions contained in the initial request
5. risks created by missing information
6. functional and non-functional areas that require investigation
7. recommended discovery outputs

Organise the questions under:
- business objectives
- applicant eligibility
- grant programs
- application processing
- assessment and approval
- document management
- security and privacy
- reporting
- accessibility
- support and operations

Identify all open questions explicitly. Do not invent answers.
